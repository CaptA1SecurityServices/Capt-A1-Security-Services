## Restoration trial

Built-in image generation was used to test a separate restoration of each photo. The results reconstructed small facial and scene details. They were reviewed and deliberately excluded from the website in favour of the user's requirement to keep the photographs real. They are not referenced by any page.

Prompt used for each edit target:

> Use case: identity-preserve. Edit target: the attached original documentary photograph. Create a restrained professional photographic restoration for a real company's website. Preserve the exact scene, people count, faces, identity, body proportions, clothing, uniforms, equipment, background, objects, activity, and original framing/aspect ratio. Only correct white balance and exposure gently, recover shadow detail, reduce compression haze/noise, and lightly sharpen existing details. Keep natural skin texture and authentic working conditions. No new faces/details, no beautification of people, no added uniforms, no removed clutter/people, no invented activity, no background replacement, no text or borders, no stylization, no oversaturation. This is evidence of real work: factual fidelity matters more than polish. Return one edited photograph.
