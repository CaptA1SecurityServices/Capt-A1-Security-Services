Our story

Built on experience. Driven by responsibility.

Security is not just about having people on-site. It is about having the right people, the right standards, and the right support behind them.

Veteran-led, Captain A1 brings a disciplined and experienced approach to security manpower and workforce solutions. We understand each site before selecting personnel, preparing them for the role, and supporting them throughout the assignment.
