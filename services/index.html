Services and site-assessment FAQs

Which security services are available in Ajmer and Jaipur?

Captain A1 plans security guard, industrial, residential, commercial and event deployments around the site, duty hours and supervision needs. Gunmen and PSOs are considered for suitable assignments, subject to legal and role requirements. Availability is confirmed after discussion.

Can I combine security guards with housekeeping?

Yes. Request both services in the service-plan form. Facility roles depend on location, shifts, supervision and contract size. Scope and availability are confirmed after discussion.

Is workforce management available?

Workforce Management is coming soon and is not currently available for booking.

Can I request equipment or CCTV installation?

Equipment & Site Readiness is coming soon. We are not currently accepting requests for this service.

What information is needed for a site assessment?

Share the site type, city and location, services required, approximate personnel count, shift hours and expected start date. Add your contact details and current challenges so the team can discuss the requirement.
