An assignment built around your site

Start with an area-by-area cleaning schedule.

Offices & workspaces

List workstations, meeting rooms, reception areas and circulation routes. Decide which duties happen before opening, during occupied hours or after closing to limit disruption.

Washrooms & shared amenities

Define inspection frequency, cleaning tasks, replenishment responsibility and escalation for leaks or broken fixtures. Confirm who supplies consumables and records completed checks.

Common areas & outdoor upkeep

Discuss entrances, corridors, stairs, walkways and grounds-care tasks. Agree waste collection points, the site’s disposal process and the boundary between routine upkeep and maintenance.

Facility support personnel

Housekeeping may form part of a wider manpower requirement. Describe the tasks, skills, shifts and reporting contact so office support and cleaning responsibilities can be assessed separately.

From requirement to delivery

An agreed scope before the first working day.

01

Describe the workload

Share the premises type, approximate areas, floors, washrooms and opening hours. Note occupied zones, busy periods and any restrictions on cleaning activities.

02

Agree people & supplies

Discuss personnel, duty hours, tools, chemicals, protective equipment and consumables. Confirm which items your site supplies and which, if any, are included in the quotation.

03

Set daily checks

Agree attendance reporting, task checklists, a site contact and review responsibilities. Discuss replacement coordination and how additional work will be requested.

Before you request a quote

Make housekeeping quotations comparable.

Provide a list of areas and tasks, expected frequency, hours, approximate personnel requirement and start date. Ask for the quotation to state manpower, materials, equipment, supervision and exclusions clearly. A daily housekeeping arrangement and a one-time specialist cleaning job can require different resources and pricing.

Common questions.

Is this suitable for ongoing office housekeeping?

You can discuss ongoing office and shared-area duties. Agree the cleaning schedule, personnel, working hours, supplies and supervision before confirming the arrangement.

Are cleaning materials and equipment included?

Their inclusion depends on the agreed quotation. Confirm tools, chemicals, consumables and protective equipment explicitly rather than assuming they are included with housekeeping manpower.

Can I book a one-time deep clean?

Describe the task, area and condition first. This page covers housekeeping personnel and agreed site-support duties; specialist deep cleaning is assessed separately, and availability must be confirmed.

How are attendance and missed duties handled?

Agree a reporting contact, attendance arrangements, task checks and replacement coordination in the service scope. Tell the team about your operating hours and any priority areas.

Can security guards and housekeeping work at the same site?

Yes, you can enquire about both. Each role needs separate duties and supervision arrangements. Use the service-plan form to describe the combined requirement.
