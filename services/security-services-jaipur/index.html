Plan the duties around your premises.

Offices & commercial premises

Set out opening and closing routines, employee access, visitor registration and reception coordination. Identify which checks belong to the guard and which remain with your office team.

Housing societies & apartments

Agree visitor and delivery procedures, resident communication, parking access and common-area patrols with the society or property manager. Include an escalation contact for every shift.

Factories & warehouses

Map vehicle entry, dispatch hours, contractor access and material movement. Define the authorization needed for inward and outward goods, gate records and shift handovers.

Events & temporary cover

Common questions.

Can I request security guards for a Jaipur housing society?

Yes. Share the society location, entrances, occupancy, visitor procedures and required shifts. The team will discuss duty instructions, supervision and personnel availability before confirming the scope.

How are guard charges decided?

Charges depend on duty hours, staffing, assignment duration, role requirements and supervision. Request a written scope so quotations can be compared on the same duties and coverage.

Can I arrange night guards or short-term security?

You can enquire about night shifts, temporary cover and events. Give the dates and exact location; staffing and supervision arrangements must be confirmed before an assignment is accepted.

Are gunmen or personal security officers available?

Specialist assignments are considered separately, subject to lawful licensing, documentation, role requirements and availability. Describe the purpose and duties so the team can assess the request.

Can housekeeping be included with security?

You can request both. Security and housekeeping responsibilities, personnel, hours and reporting arrangements are agreed separately so each role has a clear scope.
