Before you get started.

Which facility roles can I request?

You can discuss housekeeping, valet services, office support and facility manpower. Availability depends on role, location, shifts, supervision needs and contract size.

Can facility staff be combined with security?

Yes. Ask for a combined requirement in the service-plan form. Each role and its responsibilities are agreed separately before deployment.

What is included in a housekeeping service?

The scope is agreed for your site. List the rooms and shared areas, cleaning tasks, frequency, working hours and expected standards. This helps distinguish ongoing housekeeping manpower from a one-time specialist cleaning requirement.

Are cleaning materials and equipment included?

Confirm this in the quotation. Personnel, cleaning consumables, equipment, uniforms and supervision should each have a clear responsible party. Do not assume specialist machinery or materials are included unless agreed.

How are housekeeping and manpower charges decided?

We discuss the areas and tasks, staff numbers, shifts, location, contract duration and supervision. Share these details to request a quotation; the website does not promise a standard price for every site.

How are attendance and replacement requirements handled?

Agree the attendance record, reporting contact and replacement coordination before work starts. Discuss how an absence or change in workload should be reported and reviewed for your assignment.

Can I request staff in Pushkar or elsewhere in Rajasthan?

Share the exact site and role requirements. We serve Ajmer, Jaipur and other cities in Rajasthan (on request), subject to availability and supervision needs. Find our Ajmer, Jaipur and Pushkar office information on the contact page.
