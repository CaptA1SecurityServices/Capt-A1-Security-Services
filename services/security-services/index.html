Before you get started.

How many guards does my site need?

The number depends on entrances, activity, operating hours, patrol areas and shift coverage. We discuss these conditions before recommending a deployment.

Can you provide armed personnel?

Gunmen and personal security officers are considered for suitable assignments, subject to lawful licensing, documentation, role requirements and availability.

How are security guard charges calculated?

A quotation depends on the site, number of duty posts, shift hours, assignment duration, role requirements and supervision. Share these details so we can discuss a clear scope and quote. An enquiry does not confirm a fixed rate or availability.

Can I request night guards or temporary event cover?

Yes, you can discuss night-shift, temporary and event-based requirements. Tell us the dates, hours, location and duties. Staffing and supervision arrangements are confirmed before accepting an assignment.

How do I arrange security in Ajmer, Jaipur or Pushkar?

Share your location and site requirements with our operations team. Our office details are on the contact page. We serve Ajmer, Jaipur and other cities in Rajasthan (on request); each assignment depends on personnel availability and supervision needs.
