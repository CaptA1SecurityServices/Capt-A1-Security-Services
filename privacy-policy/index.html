Privacy Policy

How we handle the information you share when you contact us, apply for a role or submit a lead form.

Effective date: 26 September 2026

1. Who this policy covers

Captain A1 Security Services ("we", "us" or "our") provides security and facility manpower services in Ajmer, Jaipur and other cities in Rajasthan (on request). This policy explains our handling of personal information from visitors to captaina1.com, prospective clients, recruitment applicants and people who submit our Facebook or Instagram Instant Forms.

It covers initial enquiries and recruitment follow-up. Employee onboarding and employment records are a separate stage: any additional information needed at that stage should be explained when it is requested.

2. Information we collect

  • Meta lead forms: our current beta Facebook and Instagram form asks for full name, email address and phone number.
  • Client enquiries: website forms ask for contact details and service requirements. Depending on the form, these include name, phone number, email address, organisation, site type, city or site address, services required, personnel numbers, shift, expected start date and the message you provide.
  • Recruitment applications: the website guard-application form asks for name, mobile number and qualification level. Follow-up conversations may include information you choose to provide about your application and availability.
  • Communications: information you send by email, phone or WhatsApp, together with enquiry notes, follow-up status and meeting arrangements.
  • Website use: technical and interaction information associated with hosting and measurement tools, as described below.

The Meta lead form does not ask for Aadhaar, PAN, bank details or medical documents. These are not required for an initial website enquiry or guard application either. Please do not include such documents or account credentials in enquiry messages.

3. How we use your information

We use information to respond to your enquiry, understand and discuss service requirements, prepare follow-up or quotation discussions, assess initial recruitment interest, arrange calls or meetings, and maintain a record of the conversation. We also use website interaction information to understand how visitors find and use the site and to measure advertising responses.

We may contact you using the details you provide about the enquiry or application you submitted. Submitting a form does not automatically authorize unrelated marketing. If a separate marketing permission is requested, its purpose should be explained separately.

4. Access and service providers

Access to enquiry and application records is limited to authorized staff who need it for the relevant follow-up, recruitment, operations or administration, and service providers supporting those activities.

  • Website forms: submissions are sent through Web3Forms for delivery to our business. Email and communications providers support our responses.
  • Meta and the planned Zoho workflow: Facebook and Instagram receive information submitted within their Instant Forms. We plan to use Zoho LeadChain to transfer those leads into Zoho CRM, where authorized staff can manage the lead record, follow-up notes and meeting arrangements. This describes the intended integration, not a claim that it is already active.
  • Website and communications services: GitHub Pages hosts the website and Cloudflare supports its delivery and protection. Google tools support advertising and website measurement. WhatsApp handles messages you choose to send through it.

We do not sell information submitted through Meta lead forms. We use providers handling those leads to support the purpose for which you submitted them, not to authorize their unrelated marketing.

Relevant providers process information as part of their services, under their own terms and privacy notices. We may also disclose information where required by applicable law or a lawful request from an authority.

The public website supports HTTPS to protect information in transit. Access restrictions and the safeguards of the services used also matter; no website or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

If you use the service-plan WhatsApp button, the details entered in that plan are included in the prefilled WhatsApp link. Review the message before sending it.

5. Cookies, analytics and advertising

The website loads Google Tag Manager and Google Ads tags. These tools can use cookies or similar identifiers and process information such as page visits, browser or device information, referral information and interactions with the website to measure visits and advertising responses.

Our website code records interactions such as service selections, selected city, form-start and submission events, and clicks on phone, email and WhatsApp links. The custom interaction events in that code do not include the name, phone number, email address or free-text enquiry message entered into a form.

A Meta Pixel PageView tag is configured in Tag Manager for advertising measurement. Whether a tag runs can depend on browser settings and website security controls. This is separate from information you deliberately submit in a Meta Instant Form.

Our contact page embeds Google Maps. Loading a map connects your browser to Google. Hosting and delivery providers may process technical request information, such as IP address, requested page and browser details, to operate and protect their services.

The website does not currently provide a cookie-preference panel. You can manage or delete cookies through your browser and adjust advertising preferences in your Google or Meta account. Blocking cookies or third-party content may affect maps or other features. These controls do not delete information you have already submitted in a form.

For provider information, see Google's privacy policy, Meta's privacy policy and Zoho's privacy policy.

6. Retention and deletion

We retain enquiry, application and follow-up information for as long as needed for the purpose for which it was collected, including relevant follow-up, and for any applicable record-keeping or legal requirements. The appropriate period depends on the record and whether the enquiry or application becomes an ongoing service or employment relationship.

You can ask us to delete information that is no longer needed. We will review the request against the purpose of the record and any applicable requirements to retain it. A request does not necessarily mean that every record can be deleted immediately, including records held separately by a platform under its own policy.

To ask about your information, request a correction or deletion, raise a privacy concern, or ask us to stop enquiry or recruitment follow-up, contact Captain A1 Security Services:

8. Updates to this policy

We may update this policy when our forms, services or information-handling practices change. The current version and effective date will appear on this page. Please review it when submitting new information.
